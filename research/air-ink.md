# Riset — air-ink (Air-Ink & Kaalink, Graviky Labs, Bengaluru)

Dikumpulkan 26 Nov 2026 01:05 WIB (produksi harian). Satu fasilitas: **Graviky Labs, Bengaluru (India)** — perangkat Kaalink dipasang di knalpot kendaraan pribadi & genset di Bengaluru, jelaga diolah di lab Graviky jadi tinta AIR-INK.
Hook topics.md ("Pulpen ini tintanya dari asap knalpot mobil") disesuaikan: produk utamanya **spidol** (marker 2–50 mm) → "Tinta spidol ini… dibuat dari asap knalpot!". ## Sumber
1. "Recycling air pollution to make art" — MIT News / MIT Sustainability (Rob Matheson, 28 Nov 2017): Kaalink silinder, listrik statis, 85–95 %, 15–20 hari, kartrid dikosongkan ke unit pengumpul, logam berat & racun dibuang, 1 ons ≈ 1 spidol ≈ 45 menit. https://sustainability.mit.edu/article/recycling-air-pollution-make-art
2. "This Incredible Device Can Turn Air Pollution into Usable Ink" — Interesting Engineering: saluran menyempit, jelaga terjebak di antara pelat, partikel 1/30 lebar rambut, sensor aliran & panas, katup ventilasi elektronik saat tekanan balik, lampu biru → merah = penuh, pemisahan gravitasi → aktivasi → digiling seragam. https://interestingengineering.com/innovation/this-incredible-device-can-turn-air-pollution-into-usable-ink
3. "Graviky Labs captures air pollution and turns it into ink" / "AIR-INK" — Dezeen & designboom (2017): jelaga dimurnikan → pigmen karbon, digiling jadi bubuk, dicampur pelarut & minyak dengan takaran berbeda → tinta encer/kental; spidol 2, 15, 30, 50 mm; 45 menit = 1 fl oz = 1 pena. https://www.designboom.com/technology/graffiky-labs-air-ink-from-pollution-02-08-2017/
4. "Inking solutions to pollution" — Down To Earth (India): proses 3 tahap (tangkap → proses kimia buang logam berat & karsinogen → pigmen jadi tinta), Kaalink dipasang skala kecil di Bengaluru pada kendaraan pribadi & genset, 95 % PM. https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/air/inking-solutions-to-pollution-58398
5. "Chasing Genius: Air-Ink" — National Geographic (Jul 2017): 30 mL tinta = 45 menit polusi knalpot; bubuk hitam dicampur pelarut → botol & spidol. https://www.nationalgeographic.com/news/2017/07/chasing-genius-air-ink-carbon-pollution-graviky/
6. "Smoke in your eyes" — Deccan Chronicle (Okt 2017): Kaalink memberi muatan elektrostatik positif pada partikel; bekerja ±20 hari lalu lampu merah menandai penuh; Graviky di Bengaluru. https://www.deccanchronicle.com/lifestyle/pets-and-environment/071017/smoke-in-your-eyes-the-inexhaustible-resource.html
7. "Painting with pollution" — The News Minute (Bengaluru): Kaalink = unit silinder di pipa knalpot; jelaga menempel di dinding silinder; udara sisanya dilepas → tanpa tekanan balik. https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/painting-pollution-trio-turns-your-vehicles-fumes-ink-art-48039
8. "Turning Air Pollution Into Ink" — HowStuffWorks: daripada membakar bahan bakar fosil baru untuk membuat tinta, tinta dibuat dari asap yang sudah ada; 1 pena 30 mL = 45 menit polusi diesel. https://science.howstuffworks.com/innovation/new-inventions/air-pollution-into-ink.htm
9. "This start-up claims to be recycling polluted air to make ink" — HuffPost India (Agt 2016): berkantor pusat di Bengaluru; Kaalink silinder di pipa knalpot; diuji di mobil, truk, motor, genset. https://www.huffingtonpost.in/2016/08/21/this-start-up-claims-to-be-recycling-polluted-air-to-make-ink-an_a_21454169/
